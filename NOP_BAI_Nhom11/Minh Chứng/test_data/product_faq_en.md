# SaoMai Cloud Storage - Product FAQ (synthetic test document)

**Q: What is the free storage quota?**
A: Every free account receives 15 GB of storage. The Pro plan offers 2 TB for 99,000 VND per month.

**Q: What is the maximum single file size?**
A: The maximum upload size for a single file is 5 GB on the Pro plan and 1 GB on the free plan.

**Q: How long are deleted files kept?**
A: Deleted files stay in the Trash for 30 days before being permanently removed.

**Q: Which regions host the data?**
A: Data is hosted in two data centers: Hanoi (primary) and Ho Chi Minh City (backup).
